The survey must contain at least 3 questions covering the following topics: dietary restrictions or preferences (such as vegetarian, vegan, nut allergy, gluten-free, or none), any specific food allergies to be aware of, and any additional comments or requests. Use appropriate question types for each question (for example, a multiple-choice question for dietary preferences, and short-answer text questions for allergies and comments).

Part 3: Create a Notion page titled "Team Lunch Menu" documenting the catering plan.
